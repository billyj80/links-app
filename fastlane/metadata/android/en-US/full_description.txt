Links is a self-hosted services manager for Android. It gives you fast, organised access to all your home-server and self-hosted services directly from your phone.

Features:
• Add services with Tailscale, Cloudflare Tunnel, LAN and custom URL endpoints
• App shortcuts tab — launch any installed app with a single tap
• Colour-coded link pills for Tailscale, Cloudflare and LAN connections
• Compact or expanded card layout
• Full dark / light / system theme support
• Backup and restore (JSON + Service Links Backup .txt format)
• Reorder services and app shortcuts
• Custom icons for services and app shortcuts (preset library or gallery image)
• English and Polish language support
• No accounts, no tracking, no internet required for the app itself
